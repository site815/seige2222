# Meadow grass cards — v0.9.1

Status: imported and used by the v0.9.1 Shipping candidate for the `Grass` and `GrassB` nature roles. Rendered review and native-resolution measurements are in the [v0.9.1 graphics report](../../docs/game-design/GRAPHICS_PERFORMANCE_0_9_1.md). This is a performance-driven representation change, not a claim of reference-game parity.

## Why

The v0.8–v0.9 sward (`SM_MeadowSwardV081A/B`) was a 33,632-triangle Nanite assembly of photographed, alpha-masked blades. Masked materials make Nanite fall back to its programmable rasterizer; at 3840×1600 that single pass (`GPU/NaniteVisBuffer`, 12.0 ms mean in the retained v0.8.1 ground profile) exceeded the rest of the frame, and the matching far proxy added roughly 1.4 million Nanite instances across the 540 m streaming radius. Manor Lords and comparable games render grass as a handful of textured cards per clump through the ordinary masked pass with a dithered distance fade. v0.9.1 does the same.

## Pipeline

1. [Tools/prepare_grass_cards_v091.py](../../Tools/prepare_grass_cards_v091.py) (bundled Blender, background): places one Bermuda turf plus five overlapping tufts per cell, renders eight such groups orthographically with an unlit emission material (straight alpha, Standard view transform, Cycles CPU), pads colour into transparent texels, writes the 4096×2048 RGBA atlas with a minimal PNG encoder, and builds two clump meshes of nine bent cards each (36 triangles, vertex colour R = normalised height, G = per-card random phase). Cells are square; their world sizes and UV rectangles are in [Exports/grass_cards_manifest.json](Exports/grass_cards_manifest.json).
2. [Tools/import_grass_cards_v091.py](../../Tools/import_grass_cards_v091.py) (editor commandlet, legacy FBX route for vertex colours): imports the atlas with alpha-coverage-preserving mips, builds `M_GrassCardV091` and imports the clumps **with Nanite disabled**. The material is masked, two-sided foliage, world-space normal blended 70% toward the instance up vector, with:
   - albedo calibrated like the terrain layers: atlas colour divided by its measured mean, multiplied by an authored `GrassAlbedo`, then the same macro photograph and coherent meadow vigor modulation that `M_TerrainV08` applies, so clumps match the ground beneath them;
   - per-instance custom data 0 = meadow vigor (written by `ASeigeGameMode::CreateGroundCover`);
   - per-instance random brightness/hue variation;
   - a dithered mask from `PerInstanceFadeAmount` (interleaved gradient noise, jittered by frame) so instances fade out between the component's start and end cull distances instead of popping;
   - a two-tone wind world-position offset weighted by card height (shadow cache stays rigid; the offset is evaluated within 60 m).
3. Runtime ([SeigeTerrain.cpp](../../Source/Seige/SeigeTerrain.cpp)): non-Nanite sward sets get fade start/end cull distances per authored band, `grass_far_proxy=false` disables the far proxy entirely, and the stream radius is 200 m. Nothing is drawn beyond the fade.

The [import report](import_report.json) records the imported paths, dimensions and triangle counts. `Source/Grass_Cards.blend` is the saved Blender scene of the last bake and `Renders/cell_*.png` are the eight atlas cells. The exported FBX clumps are regenerated by the prepare script and, like the other `Exports/*.fbx`, are not tracked in Git.

## Limits

- Cards are impostors: silhouettes repeat (8 cells) and look flat at very low camera angles; the old sward had true blade geometry.
- No LOD; a clump keeps 36 triangles until it fades. Triangle count is not the cost; masked pixel overdraw is.
- Wind does not move the cached shadow.
- Beyond the fade distance the meadow relies on the terrain material alone.
