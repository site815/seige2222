# SEIGE — Third-party Asset Attribution

The following photographic texture maps were downloaded from Poly Haven for the realistic environment update. Poly Haven licenses these assets under CC0 1.0 and permits redistribution, including in commercial products. Asset pages and exact download URLs are recorded below and in the source manifest. No paid assets are used.

[Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

All texture files are stored locally and imported into packaged Unreal assets. The game does not access Poly Haven or require a network connection.
