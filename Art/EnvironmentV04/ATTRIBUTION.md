The tree is honestly identified as a Jacaranda, not an oak or hornbeam. It is 19.47 m tall with a 24.42 × 19.15 m mature crown. It is not an enlarged shrub. The grass clump measures approximately 68 × 72 × 27 cm. Both have centered horizontal bounds and ground Z=0 pivots. The source polygons, material slots, photographic UVs, and complete tree canopy are retained. The original trunk's painted transition to repeating branch bark is baked into face material assignments and UVs. Runtime uses Nanite PreserveArea, masked two-sided foliage, a 4K tree leaf color/alpha atlas, alpha mip coverage preservation, and local material mip biases. Foliage and meadow albedo multipliers calibrate the source palette to the scene; downloaded maps are unchanged. No wind animation or whole-tree billboard is supplied.

The denser meadow alternatives measure **140 × 143 × 56 cm** and **137 × 131 × 52 cm**, each with **77,572 source triangles**. [prepare_meadow_swards.py](../../Tools/prepare_meadow_swards.py) combines 16 complete tall tufts with **920 short Bermuda tufts** as a low, three-dimensional underlayer. The original tall-patch footprint and height are unchanged. Both source atlases retain their photographic UVs and use separately padded RGB derivatives with the same calibrated grass shader. This increases the source geometry budget from 28,512 triangles; actual runtime performance must be measured. Their review uses physical uneven ground and close/elevated cameras; coverage depends on runtime density and slope alignment, not just the texture.

`SM_WildflowerPatch` is **original project work**, created by [create_meadow_wildflowers.py](../../Tools/create_meadow_wildflowers.py) from curved stem tubes, shaped leaves, and petal surfaces. The 109 × 91 × 57 cm patch has 3,000 triangles with muted cream, dusky violet, and occasional yellow flowers. No reference game's assets were copied. Project licensing applies to this original geometry and its solid-color materials; it is not automatically assigned the downloaded assets' CC0 license.

The new terrain material is `/Game/Art/NatureV04/M_TerrainV04`. Its vertex channels are **R soil/path, G rock, B forest density**: blue zero is meadow, blue one is forest floor. Maps are sampled in world centimeters, with two offset detail frequencies and broad continuous variation. The ambientCG meadow source represents 140 cm per tile. Forest, path, and rock material scales are artistic physical-scale settings of 300, 300, and 250 cm. The retained source height map is not used for runtime displacement; terrain relief comes from the terrain mesh.

[sources.json](sources.json) retains exact official downloads, provider metadata, checksums and source roles. The unmodified large downloads are reproducible under ignored `.tools/nature-downloads`. Editable derivatives are stored in [Source](Source); generated FBXs are ignored and can be recreated with [prepare_environment_v04.py](../../Tools/prepare_environment_v04.py). The [export manifest](Exports/environment_manifest.json) records measured sizes, original triangle counts, material slots, and export hashes. [import_environment_v04.py](../../Tools/import_environment_v04.py) imports local maps and meshes, then creates the [terrain material](../../Tools/import_terrain_v04.py). Import and fresh-load verification reports record the saved Unreal assets. The original v0.3 assets remain available as fallbacks.

For a complete rebuild, run `prepare_environment_v04.py`, then `prepare_meadow_swards.py`, then `create_meadow_wildflowers.py` in Blender. For an incremental meadow update, [import_meadow_swards.py](../../Tools/import_meadow_swards.py) imports only the two swards, wildflower patch, and their materials/maps, preserving the mature trees.

The review images in [Previews](Previews) are local renders of the prepared assets. They are geometry/material inspection images, not screenshots of the game or claims that the entire environment is complete. Final integration and performance are checked in the game.

Runtime meadow calibration uses [pad_meadow_albedo.py](../../Tools/pad_meadow_albedo.py) in Blender to extend the Grass Medium 02 photograph's RGB beyond its unchanged alpha silhouette. This prevents black atlas background from darkening distant blade mips. The original photograph is unchanged; the separately saved derivative and its hash are recorded in [meadow_atlas_padding.json](meadow_atlas_padding.json). Run this preparation before a complete import. [calibrate_meadow_materials.py](../../Tools/calibrate_meadow_materials.py) creates a dedicated `M_V04Grass` parent with matched color/alpha mip bias, less dark tint, and thin-leaf transmission; it leaves the tree material untouched. [calibrate_environment_ground.py](../../Tools/calibrate_environment_ground.py) applies the grass and terrain changes without reimporting geometry. Fine surface detail remains physically scaled, with 24 m broad variation and stronger soil coverage driven by the runtime's shared sparse-sward mask. Terrain and grass atlas maps stay resident because absolute-world texture coordinates do not match procedural-mesh streaming estimates. Filtered mip selection remains enabled, and no emissive lighting substitute is used. The meadow remains subject to actual game capture review rather than Blender-only approval.

An additional 8 m photographic surface layer supplies medium-distance variation when the small grass blades filter down to their distant mips. It reuses the licensed Leafy Grass maps: only desaturated value variation is applied to base color, so enlarged leaf colors do not appear on the ground. Medium normals are combined with the fine normals using reoriented normal mapping, and the source roughness contributes modestly. This layer is an artistic surface-detail treatment, suppressed on rock and forest floor; it does not claim new geometry or height displacement.

[terrain_luminance_analysis.json](terrain_luminance_analysis.json) records the linear-sRGB source measurements used for the medium layer: mean luminance 0.2499 and standard deviation 0.0265 after representative 64×64 filtering. Its value response is centered on 0.25 with contrast 4, retaining the bounded 0.86–1.14 multiplier. [bermuda_atlas_padding.json](bermuda_atlas_padding.json) records the second source atlas derivative. [import_dense_meadow.py](../../Tools/import_dense_meadow.py) imports the denser swards and measured terrain update together without touching the tree models.
