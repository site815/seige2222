# Physical geography and water — v0.10.6

The shared [physical profile](../../Rules/environment.json) now authors three downhill rivers crossing all nine neighborhood regions, three connected lakes with irregular polygon shorelines, broad drainage valleys, and five oriented rock escarpments. River control points continue beyond the map edges; visible surfaces are clipped at the terrain boundary instead of starting as round pools. Coordinates and heights use simulation units (currently 0.06 m each). These are reproducible authored landforms, not an erosion or watershed simulation.

[SeigeEnvironment](../../Source/Seige/Simulation/SeigeEnvironment.cpp) supplies the same water, valley and scarp geometry to terrain and movement. Bounded Hermite reach sampling rounds river bends while preserving authored water levels and ford centers; generated samples are excluded from the routing waypoint graph, and physical queries reject distant reach bounds before doing distance work. A compact smooth blend of adjacent reach elevations keeps river banks continuous across nearest-segment bisectors; the actual stalled worker coordinate is covered by centimetre-scale movement regression checks. Water occupies a carved low valley rather than a raised narrow ribbon. Overlapping dry lake valleys fade away at river banks so they cannot introduce a hidden step at a shallow crossing. Scarps have long irregular fault faces, tapered ends and gentle back slopes. The three lakes use the same 128-point boundary for the rendered face, bed, wet/dry checks and routing obstacle.

Nine authored natural fords (one per region) provide roadless crossings: the gravel shelf lies about 12 cm below the continuous river water, has a finite clearance radius, and blends back into the deeper bed. The single water material uses a continuous shallow tint from physical bed depth, avoiding separate rectangular ford patches. `SegmentTraversable` admits a crossing only when every water-intersection interval fits a ford's body-clearance circle; deep water and lakes remain blocked, including long diagonal movement. `CanStand` / `SegmentDry` remain strictly dry for construction and deposits. Regional offsets are explicit and saved; deposit generation still chooses exactly three standard and two rare resources. Surface water creates no extra extractable deposits. A water deposit represents accessible groundwater with room for its mine on dry land. No bridge, freezing-water or hydraulic simulation is supplied.

The [water importer](../../Tools/import_water_v09.py) creates `/Game/Art/EnvironmentV09/M_WaterV09` and eight mineral/soil material variants. The [import report](water_import_report.json) confirms these assets were saved. Water is an original opaque, lit material with three small animated ripples, a broad irregular phase warp and screen-derivative filtering to reduce distant shimmer. Roughness is 0.48 and specular is 0.12; there is no refraction or geometric wave displacement. The river ribbon and lake face share the physical profile, and bed shaping preserves the connected wet channel where river and lake banks overlap.

Deposit geology uses the actual visible node's resource identity, including nodes in otherwise empty regions. It reuses licensed rock geometry with different outcrop proportions and mineral/soil palettes. These are readable prototype geological cues, not scientific scans of each named deposit. Rock Boulder Dry textures by Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers, and Rock Moss Set 01 geometry by Kless Gyzen, retain their recorded CC0 provenance in the [third-party register](../THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md). No new photographs or downloads were added. The water graph and authored landform profile are original project work under the project's licensing decisions.

Sector transitions now retain the seven unchanged terrain tiles and their forest components when visible foundations have not changed. Old and new detail sectors rebuild against the correct triangle heights; changed visibility/foundation signatures trigger a full refresh. Local grass currently resets and refills on this transition. This reduces redundant work but is not a claim that the boundary hitch is eliminated.

Verification for v0.10.6 includes the existing water/route, offset persistence and sector-cache tests, plus `Seige.Environment.RegionalValleysAndEscarpments`: all nine regions, real terrain below water/above banks, shared irregular shorelines, asymmetric scarp profiles, strict dry placement and physical shallow-ford traversal. `-WorldReview -TerrainReview` records five paused overview views plus three closer oblique ford/stream/scarp views without touching player saves. Final pass/fail and captures are recorded with the release verification; older v0.9 reports do not verify the revised geography.

The eight final rendered views confirm curved connected waterways, neighboring lakes, long scarp faces, and removal of the rectangular ford tint artifact. Visual polish remains limited: river widths are fairly uniform, shoreline transitions are hard/plain, and the opaque water does not reveal underwater gravel or provide foam/refraction. Rock-face textures repeat and some distant forest proxies remain angular. Close views show the detailed foliage and terrain materials; overview views intentionally use lower detail. This is improved prototype geography, not Manor Lords visual parity. The paused offscreen captures are visual evidence, not performance measurements.
