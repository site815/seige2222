# seige2222 — Vision and Setting

[Design index](README.md) · [Status definitions](README.md#design-status)

The setting, player role, and intended scope of the game's references.

**Current working name:** **seige2222**, selected by the user. SEIGE and “Robot Manor Lords” are earlier project/working labels; they do not name a separate game.

**Tentative era:** Around the year 2200 was suggested as a possibility. The date and broader world history are not finalized.

## Premise

**Confirmed:** A science-fiction settlement and colony simulation on newly settled planets. Humanity is losing a war against insect-like aliens, but civilization and industry still function. This is not a post-apocalyptic setting. The player is a local ruler who sustains a population, develops industry, holds territory and supplies, and organizes its defense.

The central design combines meaningful, visible production with physical logistics, resource-driven capabilities, territorial choices, and automatically executed combat. A colony can suffer destruction or permanent defeat; relocation can provide an escape if the player has prepared it.

**Confirmed design baseline:** Develop the concepts as a fully persistent multiplayer game. The single-player version is built first with AI opponents and the same gameplay rules, rather than a separate colony design.

**Confirmed current scope:** The colony population consists of robots. Human and mixed populations are deferred for possible future reconsideration. This limits the current population systems while retaining the wider setting of humanity's war.

**Confirmed conflict scope:** Colony-to-colony conflict currently centers on privateering, raids, and theft. Formal war against other regions is removed for now.

**Confirmed current visual direction:** A realistic, Earth-like landscape follows a Manor Lords environmental reference, with detailed, realistic futuristic buildings. Robots remain appealing and invite empathy; bugs should be menacing and realistic. This supersedes the earlier bright Pandora landscape and cute-building direction. See [Art Direction](ART_DIRECTION.md).

### References and their intended scope

- **Manor Lords:** Detailed visible workers and resources together with meaningful simulation and production. Also the primary interface reference and the current reference for a realistic, Earth-like landscape.
- **StarCraft / StarCraft II:** The minimal starting setup of a command center and workers, now also a secondary interface reference for polish. Individual-unit combat control is not adopted.
- **WALL-E / EVE:** Appealing futuristic robots that encourage player empathy. The earlier extension of this reference to cute colony buildings is superseded.
- **Pandora:** Earlier landscape exploration, superseded by the realistic Earth-like environmental direction.
- **Starship Troopers:** The insect-like alien enemy touchstone.
- **Mad Max:** Emerging fragmented power and warlords controlling land and supplies, not a collapsed industrial civilization.
- A medieval alternative was considered and deferred in favor of science fiction.
