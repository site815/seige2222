## Initial assistant catalog — proposals only

Nothing in this section is a confirmed resource list, building specification, or balanced recipe set. The catalog supplies concrete material for later discussion. Its chemistry and resource feasibility have not been validated; recipes are fictional game examples.

**Scope update:** The current population is robots only. This initial catalog predates that choice. Human-oriented uses and facilities such as food production and housing remain earlier proposals to revisit, not established robotic needs. Materials may still have industrial uses; selecting robots does not finalize or remove resource types or recipes.

A unique high-end lithography capability inside it is a possible explanation, not an added confirmed building or recipe. The established manufacturing and rate constraints are recorded in [Population, Necessities, and Morale](POPULATION_AND_MORALE.md).
