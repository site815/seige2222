# seige2222 — Art Direction

[Design index](README.md) · [Status definitions](README.md#design-status)

Visual direction for the robotic colony, landscape, and alien threat. This document records intent and references; a complete visual style and production specification have not been selected.

## Robot appearance

**Confirmed direction:** Robots should be cute and appealing enough for the player to empathize with them. The colony population should invite attachment even though it is mechanical.

**User reference:** The earlier “Eva-style” description was clarified with WALL-E / EVE as the reference for cute futuristic robots and the colony's buildings. Specific silhouettes, palettes, locomotion, and animation remain open.

The current scope is a single robotic population type. Visual variety and worker-role presentation have not yet been designed.

## Landscape and colony buildings

**Confirmed direction:**

- The world should be bright and colorful, using Pandora as a visual reference but aiming for an even brighter, more colorful presentation.
- Buildings belonging to the robotic colony should be cute and futuristic, following the WALL-E / EVE direction alongside the robots themselves.
- Alien bugs should appear menacing and realistic, creating a contrast with the appealing colony and colorful environment.

These references establish visual intent, not an exact copied asset design or a selected rendering technique. Specific environmental biomes, materials, shapes, lighting, and the degree of realism remain to be developed.

## Working name

**Current working name:** **seige2222**, selected by the user. “Robot Manor Lords” was an earlier informal suggestion, and SEIGE is the older project label still present in the workspace path.

## Open visual decisions

- Which qualities of the WALL-E / EVE and Pandora references should inform the game's own designs.
- Robot shape, scale, movement, expression, and ways of distinguishing jobs or state.
- Concrete designs for futuristic colony buildings, industrial processes, colorful terrain, fleets, and menacing aliens.
- Camera and presentation choices needed to make workers and physical goods readable.

These are areas to develop. The authorized first playable can use original procedural visuals to communicate the setting and systems; that does not finalize the production art style or authorize copying the reference properties' character designs.
