# seige2222 v0.10.4 — Scenario, movement and interface fixes

2026-10-12 · Windows x64 · Unreal Engine 5.8.3 · Local release

Building and empty-ground clicks no longer trigger the implicit return to the regional map. Construction footprints remain selectable before their finished mesh exists. Explicit region/back navigation still works, and a new game clears old camera bookmarks.

Home deposits remain charted after landing, including scenarios with background bugs and periodic attacks both disabled. Their markers and physical geology use the same visibility rule. Neighboring resources still require intelligence coverage. Each new game draws one fresh seed, captured before incremental preparation; all nine sectors retain exactly three distinct standard and two distinct rare resources inside their allowed region. Threat switches do not change the seeded geology. Saves preserve the layout; `-ScenarioSeed=815` can reproduce a scenario for development.

The automatic spare-worker reserve derives from current command crew capacity: **5 / 10 / 15** at levels one/two/three. Manual colony target buttons are removed. An unfinished upgrade grants no extra reserve, and additional trading-port worker targets remain independent and additive. These are production requests, not free worker bodies: assembly still needs its material bill, energy and time. Save validation rejects a mismatched reserve override.

One editable mobility profile per family now supplies these rates; the duplicate worker-speed field is removed. Cargo has no speed penalty. Existing terrain effects, powered road/rail/vacuum multipliers of **2× / 4× / 8×**, loading times and travel energy per distance remain. Workers travel at 8 km/h and transports at 7.5 km/h on their permitted rough terrain before other effects; grief still halves worker movement only.

Playback cycles **Paused / 1× / 10× / 20×** in both directions. Space resumes the previous running rate. The simulation and neighbor clocks follow those rates, and 20× is accepted in compatible save metadata. First-person Rex control stays at 1×. Worker assembly is nominally 600 simulation seconds at full staffing: ten real minutes at 1×, one minute at 10× or thirty seconds at 20×, plus any operational delays.

Top resource hover ledgers and nested material details now work while the build catalog stays open. They draw above the catalog and retain pointer access. The upper and lower HUD casings keep the single-row, screen-height-width layout from v0.10.3.

The audio pass makes construction placement feedback silent alongside routine interface clicks. Combat cues share a real-time cadence cap; completion feedback has a longer cooldown, and continuous damage creates one alert per incident, rearmed only after thirty quiet real seconds. Foreign projectile sounds now use their actual sector offset, and hitscan deduplication includes sector ownership. The same frame/second budget applies across UI calls and world updates, including 20× catch-up bursts. Important error, damage, completion and combat feedback remains enabled. All twelve source clips passed waveform, clipping, hash and import-reference checks; this was a code/configuration and signal review, not a subjective listening session.
