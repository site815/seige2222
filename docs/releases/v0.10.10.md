# seige2222 v0.10.10

Windows single-player prototype; Unreal Engine 5.8.3.

## Changes

- AI colonies protect actual construction and upgrade bills, retain permanent crews during charging/hauling, and pause optional processors that would consume the next development step's supplies.
- AI builds battery capacity before vehicle manufacturing, reserves energy for paid assembly, and grows or replaces its haulers through the factory. Vehicle funding and payment preserve ingredient reservations and strict save compatibility.
- Finite accepted trade deliveries now precede optional production replenishment, while essential priorities remain higher. Production buffers exclude reserved pickups, and eligible waiting builders can physically load construction cargo.
- Extractors search reachable nearby plots at the same deposit. Local routing avoids unnecessary terrain detours, and stranded deliveries can return to another reachable storage building with the same vehicle and cargo.
- Workers budget charging trips using actual terrain travel, queues and docking time, then return physically to their task. Charging decisions follow the saved simulation clock, preserving continuation after loading.
- AI favors uncovered defense approaches and an early paid tower in threat-enabled scenarios. Recovery towers increase within the authored plan after civilian losses.
- Command centers and every tower tier have visible independently aimed, omnidirectional weapon mounts. Range, power, ammunition, collision and cover still apply.
- Bug pursuit stops at building surfaces; embedded enemies walk out normally. Open construction becomes opaque projectile cover at its enclosure phase, while deliberate hits and splash can still damage it.
- Initial shuttle setup work drops from 900 to **30 simulation seconds**. Descent, hatch exit, finite workers and physical material delivery remain; other construction durations are unchanged.
- The audit fixes passive privateer engagement, idle-transport hits, reserved fuel consumption, occupied/wet wall placement, loading state and strict persistence checks. Verification also identifies actual process crashes and records real construction/trade waits.
- Retains independent zoom/tilt and third-person Rex controls: mouse steering, Shift to run and Alt + mouse free look, including AI-observation scenarios. Walk/run animations blend with movement speed.

Start a **new scenario** because Rules and AI fingerprints changed. Previous saves and local builds are preserved. The local workspace launcher is `Play-seige2222.cmd`.

## Download

Download the Windows ZIP and its `.sha256` file from this release. Verify the checksum, extract the ZIP, and run `Seige.exe`. Single-player gameplay is local and does not need a server.

## Limits

This remains a prototype. Bounded AI tests do not establish every seed or prolonged hostile survival. Persistent multiplayer and roads remain deferred. The visual changes cover weapon mounts and Rex locomotion; terrain quality, finished photorealism and sustained-FPS improvements are not established by this update. Rex's existing prototype asset-license exception and provenance remain recorded in Credits; this update does not change those terms.

Rendering validation applies to the default **DirectX 12 / Shader Model 6** path. The optional DX11/SM5 terrain shaders exceed the sampler limit for `M_Terrain`, `M_TerrainV07` and `M_TerrainV08`, so DX11 fallback is unsupported for the current terrain. Those warnings also occurred in v0.10.0/v0.10.1; the current SM6 cook succeeds and the tested Shipping process uses D3D12.

Hostile economic development is still slow: the final case completed after **32.189 simulation hours**, missing the original 18-hour objective. Extractor crews also load, haul and recharge, limiting export income. The acceptance test allows 72 simulation hours for the unchanged goal, finite starter kit and enabled threats; gameplay rates were not increased to meet that test window.
