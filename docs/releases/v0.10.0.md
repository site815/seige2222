## Changes

- Each worker has one physical identity, a permanent crew owner, a current task and a battery. Player/Starting-AI colonies begin with five workers; core crew capacities are 5/10/15. Workers physically approach charging berths and resume their work afterward.
- Only the command core manufactures workers. The former worker-factory blueprint is now a Construction Hub. Automatic-disassembly and task-priority controls are removed; colony spare-worker and trading-port stock targets remain separate.
- An autonomous transport vehicle starts aboard the shuttle. It carries 2,000 kg / 3,000 L with a 100 kWh battery, has no driver population, and uses real loading and unloading crews. Vehicle factories can build more. Combat and transport vehicles share one shuttle bay; vehicles left outside do not evacuate.
- Movement rules distinguish drones, transports, wheeled vehicles, tracked vehicles and mechs by speed, slope and clearance. Cargo and vehicle fabrication retain explicit material/energy bills. Specialist factories produce their own chassis families; the core is not a universal material or weapon factory.
- Level-1/2/3 fusion power plants join the new Energy construction category. Existing resource layers and recipes remain; rules and balance data stay outside the engine.
- A thin top row contains **Credits, Energy, Workers, Raw, Basic and Advanced**. Nested material hover shows local stock, reservations, delivery/production escrow, producers and trade quotes. Worker hover/click shows crew, activity, cargo and battery. Vehicles are counted separately from workers.
- The bottom strip combines pulse timing, date/time and colony age. Production-chain overlays preserve the underlying view, including the region map. Deselecting a focused region restores the previous camera. Build shortcuts include **B → E → F** for fusion power.
- Equipment panels enumerate the complete chassis-plus-weapons bill. Tiny prices use clearly sized quote batches and **<0.01 Cr** transaction totals instead of misleading zeroes. These are display changes; internal price precision is preserved.
- Original offline-synthesized sound effects and bounded in-world night lighting accompany the asset updates. Rex's original Blender asset and companion behavior were refined, including a bounded leash and damage-triggered retreat. His likeness remains visibly stylized.

## Saves

**Start a new colony.** Save format **8** and **prototype-10.0** rules store permanent crews, charging and autonomous transport state. Format-7 saves and mismatched Rules/AI fingerprints are rejected without overwriting existing saves. A project version label alone does not decide compatibility.
