# seige2222 v0.10.5 — Functional sound and physical colony logistics

2026-10-12 · Windows x64 · Unreal Engine 5.8.3 · **Verified local Shipping build**

Starting colonies stalled when truck routes crossed terrain their movement checks could not traverse, construction lent away essential core operators, or charging dependencies blocked physical crews. This update repairs those paths and restores useful sound feedback for actions and their outcomes.

## Changes

- Road routes connect to corridor interiors and intersections. Completed unpowered roads remain usable at base speed, and existing road service survives upgrades. Changed worker destinations and truck routes require actual arrival at the correct endpoint.
- Road surfaces follow continuous elevation profiles with shared heights at bends, branches and crossings. Foundation shoulders blend without artificial cliffs or repeated grading. Bounded terrain search finds alternatives to blocked direct routes; slope checks inspect short banks and near-limit transitions. Existing vehicle mobility limits remain in force.
- Trucks prefer nominal loading points, then reachable berths within the existing eight-meter service radius. Both travel legs and a physical worker path to a truck door are checked before reserving goods. A failed physical movement slice releases an unpicked claim; loaded cargo returns physically to its source while workers can take over the original demand. Cargo remains on the vehicle until actual unloading.
- AI retains an operating core crew during construction. Charging builders retain their crew slots; powerless unpicked claims can be released without losing held cargo. Charging checks actual power, accessible queues and attendant availability. An empty worker cannot reserve a berth it cannot reach.
- The editable AI export policy protects current bills, operating and worker buffers, and the next prerequisite instead of hoarding every future facility's cost. Ports still earn credits through paid, timed trade. No recovery teleports or free stock are introduced.
- Twelve original SFX provide distinct navigation, accepted/rejected action, construction, completion, damage and weapon feedback. Hover remains silent. Interface, world and notification playback have separate real-time budgets, so fast combat cannot consume UI feedback capacity. Repeated damage alerts are grouped into incidents.
