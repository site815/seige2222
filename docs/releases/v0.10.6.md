# seige2222 v0.10.6 — Terrain access, roadless colonies and ground stock

2026-10-17 · Windows x64 · Unreal Engine 5.8.3

**Verified Shipping build.** The launcher targets this version. GitHub delivery uses the same tested October 17 package; the October 23 delivery changes only documentation and CI.

Roads are deferred at the user's request. Colonies use physical terrain routes and their own shared electricity grid. The update also repairs delivery stalls, revises water and cliffs, keeps distant trees moving, and makes outdoor stockpiles inspectable.

## Changes

- Road construction, upgrades and shortcuts are removed from the catalog. AI creates no roads or connector prerequisites; disabled legacy roads provide no speed bonus and claim no construction or repair crew. Authored road tiers remain for later work.
- Each colony shares its own generation and batteries while roads are deferred. Neighboring grids remain separate. Passive consumption, transaction costs, finite storage and defensive energy priorities still apply.
- Worker and truck assignments preflight actual access. A failed loading-worker route can release its assignment and choose reachable crew. Unreachable old construction sites and nearer disconnected stockpiles cannot permanently exclude reachable work or supply. Existing bodies and cargo are preserved; recovery does not teleport units or grant goods, charge or credits.
- A physical movement failure now invalidates the original cached route segment, preventing units from walking back and retrying it forever. Riverbank elevations blend continuously across adjacent reaches, removing the small vertical seam that stalled the seed-2222 colony. Both failures have regressions using actual movement steps.
- Starting AI builds one early charging station and postpones the other two until after component production. This changes spending order while retaining the final facility plan and finite starting kit.
- Three river valleys cross the nine-region neighborhood, with irregular lake shores and long asymmetric escarpments. Shared physical and rendered curves replace abrupt river elbows. Shallow natural gravel fords provide roadless crossings; deep water remains impassable, and wet ground remains invalid for buildings and deposits.
- Tree crowns bend more gently and remain animated at survey distance, including distant proxies. Cosmetic wind advances continuously at approximately 1× / 3.16× / 4× for game playback 1× / 10× / 20×; pause freezes it. Simulation time and movement speeds are unchanged. No FPS improvement is claimed.
- Hover visible ground goods for their resource and amount. Click to pin quantity, mass, volume, location and reserved/unclaimed stock. Construction piles show delivered materials. Empty, hidden or removed piles clear their selection; inspection never creates extra inventory.

Close surface detail, angular distant tree proxies, repeating scarp textures, terrain transition hitches and sustained performance still need work.
