# seige2222 v0.10.9 — Rex steering and locomotion

2026-10-23 · Local Windows Shipping build

- Mouse movement steers Rex, including turning while stationary. WASD moves relative to the steering direction.
- Hold Shift to run: 14 km/h, versus 5 km/h walking. World playback stays at 1×.
- Hold Alt and move the mouse to look around without redirecting Rex. Releasing Alt smoothly returns the camera to the steering direction; Shift and Alt can be held together.
- Acceleration, braking and bounded turns replace instant starts and heading snaps. Physical strides check terrain, water, buildings and the existing territory boundary.
- New planted-paw walk and running-trot clips blend with idle using actual movement speed. Heading, speed and traveled distance interpolate between simulation ticks; the body follows local slopes. Rex's mesh and photographic coat are retained.
