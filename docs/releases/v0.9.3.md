## Changes

- Complete industry/building kit, level-specific campuses, tower/factory/wall meshes, rendered portraits and their material dependencies are delivered with the source. Higher core levels surround the retained upright shuttle.
- Terrain and forest uploads are spread across frames. Scene readiness includes outstanding terrain, forest and deferred sector work; screenshots and benchmarks reject incomplete scene preparation.
- Adjacent building plots preserve their own level foundations. Health-driven scorch, smoke, fire and ruins are integrated; destroyed buildings extinguish emissive lamps and windows.
- Integration fixes resolve the forest-state compilation blocker and make the shuttle-selection test handle missing or unprojectable hulls safely.
- Rex uses an original, locally authored procedural fallback. The restricted generated mesh and photo-baked coat are retired from the current release tree. Companion gameplay and animations remain; photographic likeness is unfinished. Prior Git history is unchanged; see [provenance](../../Art/CompanionDog/TEXTURE_PROVENANCE.md).
- Release verification now checks complete native-test coverage and explicit validator success, and binds packaged gameplay to the executable, cooked containers, source/runtime assets and staged JSON. Missing endpoint observations cannot pass as offline evidence.

## Saves and limits

Save format remains **7**, with rules **prototype-9.3**. Changing only the project version does not invalidate a save. Valid saves require matching rules versions and parsed Rules/AI content fingerprints; incompatible edited rules or older formats are rejected. This policy does not claim every historical save was tested.

Long-term AI balance, varied-seed recovery, crowded-colony performance, terrain/material repetition, foliage transitions and Rex likeness still need work. No Manor Lords visual parity or sustained 60 FPS claim is made. Persistent multiplayer, general inter-colony trade and full relocation remain later work.
