# seige2222 v0.10.3 — Unit inspection and Rex's shuttle lifecycle

2026-10-11 · Windows x64 · Unreal Engine 5.8.3 · Local release

Vehicles now use the same visible-model hover and click inspection as workers. Transport inspectors show health, battery, cargo capacity, cargo and delivery ownership. Fleet vehicles retain fleet commands while exposing their own loadout, shield, armor and cargo. Picking uses the visible actor, terrain occlusion and owning sector; hidden, embarked and dead bodies do not provide phantom targets.

All exposed units show an actual HP number and bar above their model on hover. Buildings show HP above and a construction percentage/bar below; completed structures show Built 100%. Workers now have persistent health and participate in direct and splash damage. Killed couriers and cargo handlers release their assignments through the existing physical ownership ledger.

The top HUD and bottom dock share a centered width equal to the screen's height: 900 physical pixels at 1600×900 and 1600 at 3840×1600. The bottom dock has one row: Build, Regions, Chain, Objectives, Rex, Menu, Pause, Speed, and the complete timer. The Rex button focuses him or his shuttle while he is inside.

Rex begins aboard and physically exits after landing opens the hatch. He roams within 100 m of the orbital module. The provisional rules give him 25 HP, a 35 m danger radius, 14 km/h retreat speed and 5 HP/second healing only inside the shuttle. He returns outside when healed and the danger has cleared for 60 simulation seconds. Death is permanent for that colony and slows every worker's movement to 50% for exactly 30 calendar days. Pause, speed changes and save/load preserve that deadline. The Rex panel shows health, shelter state and remaining mourning time; first-person roaming remains available only safely outside at 1×.

Focusing Rex cancels active wall placement and fleet targeting. A fleet movement correction also makes defensive firing positions use the same clearance as their approach routes, so guards can reach the positions they choose beside buildings.

**Save compatibility:** format 9 adds mandatory worker/companion health, Rex lifecycle state and the calendar deadline. The changed Rules fingerprint also prevents loading prior releases. Start a new scenario; existing save files are left intact and are not silently migrated. The rule-set label remains `prototype-10.0`; compatibility depends on format and exact fingerprints, not that display label alone.
