# Scenario colony AI

These editable definitions control the simple local AI colonies and the center colony in observer mode. `Rules` remains the authority for construction costs, staffing, production, deliveries, repairs, and alien threats.

**Current v0.8 source:** AI colonies use the same `prototype-8.0` phased construction, reserved square plots, routed cargo/crews, worker support, power and external trade as the player. The controller lands beside a real generated standard deposit, connects facilities with paid road construction, exports one nearby renewable raw material and imports missing inputs. Each occupied region has a distinct deterministic seed and only three standard plus two rare deposits. Format-5 scenarios require current Rules and AI fingerprints; previous saves are not migrated. The current ten native AI tests pass cleanly: the exact-neighbor regression and the nine remaining AI tests report zero warnings or failures. Developed seed 815 reached all 20 required installations at 55,650 simulated seconds; the previously failing neighbor seed -2027807669 reached them at 53,920 seconds, both within the unchanged 64,800-second preparation budget. The fresh starting colony also passed its survival and authored production objective. Evidence: `Saved/Automation/v08-independent-neighbor/index.json` and `Saved/Automation/v08-independent-ai-remainder/index.json`. Current rendered/package verification remains separate and pending. See [Workers, Construction and Transport 0.8](../docs/game-design/CONSTRUCTION_AND_TRANSPORT_0_8.md).

- [colony_ai.json](colony_ai.json) sets decision cadence, ordered building targets, placement search, sensor extension, guard charging thresholds, finite setup limits and trade batch sizes. Repeated targets may raise earlier counts. Extractor targets apply only to the chosen nearby standard resource; unavailable or remote raw types do not block the plan. The opening establishes solar power, support, one sensor and one turret before the local extractor and trading port. A worker factory then supports further defense and industry. Each stage waits for completion and staffing; paid roads connect its actual access points before expansion continues.
- [developed_start.json](developed_start.json) supplies finite core inventory and population for the developed scenario option. Setup runs the actual simulation until required buildings finish within `developed_setup_seconds` and the action limit. Couriers, builders, services, upkeep, production and threats all apply. Preparation time and manufactured output are real, not preset awards.

`decision_scheduling_policy` selects how the configured decision cadence is allocated. `independent_tactics_trade_construction` updates the existing guard fleet, attempts one ordinary paid port transaction, and then permits up to `max_actions_per_decision` construction actions, including road links. A changed guard order or a new road therefore cannot consume the trade channel. Failed purchases can still only be funded by an ordinary export of owned goods. The alternative `shared_action_budget` retains the earlier common action budget for reproducible comparisons. Both use the same cadence, prices, finite inventories, physical delivery and construction prerequisites; neither grants extra units or materials. The same-decision guard deployment and paid import regression passes, as does full developed-neighbor readiness within the original deadline.

Preparation logs report new highs in completed, staffed and connected target counts, plus occasional summaries. `Diagnostics.DevelopedContinuation` is an opt-in diagnostic outside the normal `Seige` acceptance group: it requires a failed snapshot path and an absolute simulation-hour limit, resumes the old shared scheduling policy, and still checks every developed target. Its longer diagnostic horizon does not change the authored scenario preparation deadline or establish release acceptance.

Rendered scenarios containing developed colonies prepare a separate candidate across frames, with a progress screen and cancellation. Each frame targets a six-millisecond preparation budget, checked between fixed simulation steps; loading one definition set and committing the completed scene are separate bounded operations. An individual simulation step is not interrupted. The active center and neighbors are replaced together only when the complete candidate succeeds. Cancellation or failure discards the candidate and preserves the current colony. Total preparation time can still be substantial because the paid development is actually simulated; progress is not an estimated completion time. Synchronous `Initialize` remains available for headless tests and uses the same fixed-step preparation logic. The incremental determinism regression passes against the original ten-second stepping reference. Rendered preparation and cancellation remain part of the separate interface/package verification.

A starting AI uses the ordinary scenario start. Both types then use the same controller: choose nearby matching deposits, extend live coverage when required, establish production and defenses, and rebuild missing target buildings when affordable. Orders use normal simulation commands. Workers, inputs, maintenance, delivery time and attacks retain their normal effects. The controller waits for sensor workers rather than repeatedly buying unstaffed masts.

`support_recovery_policy` restores lost support capacity before ordinary expansion when jobs or active population exceed the operating capacity. It re-enables an existing service bay or pays for a missing bay already included in the configured target counts, and waits for pending recovery construction rather than buying duplicates. This breaks the dependency cycle where a replacement worker factory waits for staff while a later support target is needed to activate those workers. Recovery still needs delivered materials, assigned construction crews and a real power connection. `Seige.AI.WorkerSupportRecovery` passes this capacity shortage through ordinary paid construction and distinguishes a destroyed facility from a temporary power outage.

Defense and radar placement searches use a compact ring around the command core, oriented toward the one exploited resource and distributed around the planned perimeter. They do not shift the search origin toward unrelated unused deposits. Normal plot clearance, coverage, construction costs and road connectivity still constrain every candidate; the search can expand only within its configured bounds.

The AI deploys its finite carried guard fleet to defend paid construction. A visible enemy approaching an existing installation takes priority over a newer site. Guards receive a stationary `defense` mission, leaving convoy-following `escort` as a distinct behavior. The combat system handles target acquisition and finding a clear firing position; the AI does not read hidden enemy contacts or grant defensive units.

`guard_service` currently orders a return when any living guard falls below 30% battery. The fleet physically moves to the core access point with passive aggression, then stays until every living member reaches 90%. Charging draws from the real local grid through the ordinary combat service system; moving home, waiting and charging still take time. Passive aggression avoids expending charging energy on continuous fire or moving away to flank. The fleet resumes defensive orders afterward. The saved ordinary mission, destination and aggression retain this hysteresis across loading; no separate unsaved AI service state exists. Colony construction and trading decisions continue while guards recharge. `Seige.AI.GuardServiceAndCompactCoverage` passes paid sensor placement, physical charging, service persistence and return to defense.

This is a deterministic settlement controller with external trade. The new combat/fleet module does not automatically add strategic fleet production or rival raiding to this AI plan. Diplomacy and direct neighboring-colony trade remain outside its scope. Colonies retain independent local inventories, grids, credit accounts and threats. The developed seed is a finite scenario choice, not recurring AI income; every export debits actual goods and every import spends earned credits.

The verified v0.7 package adds independent scenario switches for background bugs and periodic attacks, both ON by default. The optional trailing initialization parameters preserve the previous behavior for existing callers.
