# Scenario colony AI

These editable definitions control local AI colonies and the center colony in observer mode. `Rules` remains the authority for live costs, individual workers, production, transport, power, repairs and threats.

## Starting and established scenarios

A **Starting AI** uses the player's finite landing manifest: one deploying shuttle, five carried workers, zero credits and the authored 9.57-tonne material kit. It constructs, staffs and supplies new facilities through ordinary paid actions. The player landing is unchanged.

A **Developed AI** starts from the explicit established state in [developed_start.json](developed_start.json). It does not simulate hours of colony growth while loading. The current provisional manifest contains **24 buildings total: one command core and 23 completed facilities**, 43 assigned operators and no idle workers, four stored worker bodies, 0.5 Galactic credits, 145 kWh in local batteries, and bounded inventories in named buildings. Inventories total 7,936 kg and 5,515.8 litres, including the four packed 80 kg workers; active bodies, installed structures and the carried unarmed cargo vehicle are separate assets. The manifest supplies no combat fleet. These are authored scenario starting assets, not recurring income or grants during play.

The manifest's 60-second colony age marks an already deployed settlement. It is not claimed as simulated construction history and does not advance the shared world calendar. Produced/delivered statistics begin at zero. Scenario integration binds the common calendar after all region candidates succeed. Normal threat timers begin after initialization, honoring the scenario's background-bug and periodic-attack switches.

The manifest declares every building's definition, relative offset in metres, operator count, selected recipe, cargo and stored battery energy. The mine binds to one actual generated standard deposit. Fixed weapons use ordinary definition loadouts; the `unarmed_transport_manifest` fleet selector retains the single authored cargo vehicle. Deterministic candidate rotations must satisfy the same dry-land, reserved-plot and physical access geometry as live construction. All plots are present before access routes are checked. Roads are currently deferred, so no authored roads are instantiated and each colony uses its own shared electricity grid. Worker initialization creates unique physical identities at legal workstations and exact stored-body locations. Capacities, inventory units, assignments, body overlap, support, recipes and building references are validated before committing the candidate.

`BeginInitialize` may finish an established region immediately; the scenario still commits all regions atomically and permits cancellation between regions. The layout search is bounded, but initialization is not promised to fit a six-millisecond frame. Failure preserves the current colony. Consult the versioned release verification record for the exact initialization, save/load and bounded continuation checks performed on a shipped build.

The selector `colony_ai.json.developed_initialization` defaults to `established_manifest`. `simulated_history` retains the former paid-growth path for explicit diagnostics; its provisional 129,600-second ceiling and action budget are not the default scenario's acceptance criteria. The historical growth failures remain diagnostic evidence, not successful established-scenario tests. Starting AI can rebuild slowly under prolonged attacks: its finite crews also haul and charge, reducing mine throughput while replacement structures and worker bodies consume paid inputs. Choosing an established start does not claim to fix that pacing limitation.

## Live controller

Both starts then run the same ordinary controller. The ordered plan has 23 priority entries, requiring 24 facilities plus the command core (25 buildings total), with one additional optional AI-chip factory. Repeated entries increase the target count of an existing facility family. The 24-building established manifest needs to construct the mandatory vehicle factory through normal play. The plan opens with trade and one extraction mine, then solar generation, charging support, alloy and component production before sensors, towers and later industry. The construction hub adds dedicated builders; the command core remains the worker manufacturer. The large battery bank follows the material-producing chain. Buildings remain real paid construction orders with physical access and delivery after initialization. The disabled road policy creates no roads or connector prerequisites. Ordered expansion and its funding bills use permanent crew assignments: an operator temporarily charging or handling cargo does not make an established facility unfinished. Actual production, staffed generators and live sensor coverage still depend on the workers physically operating the facility. The authored core operator minimum is retained when lending workers to facilities, construction and hauling; a returning or blocked worker is not treated as an on-site replacement. When the retained operator charges, an available builder returns through the ordinary route. A powerless courier releases only unpicked source reservations; actual carried goods remain with the same body.

`decision_scheduling_policy: independent_tactics_trade_construction` updates any existing guard fleet, attempts one paid trade transaction and permits up to `max_actions_per_decision` construction actions at each cadence. `shared_action_budget` is available for comparisons. Neither policy creates units, energy or resources.

The v0.10.10 order retains early trading, extraction, one solar array, one charging station, alloys, components, a sensor and one defense tower. It then develops industrial glass, circuits, biofuel, battery storage and the vehicle factory before extra charging stations, additional perimeter guns and fuel generation. The battery bank precedes the factory because transport fabrication requires a 60 kWh transaction, exceeding the core plus one solar array's 35 kWh capacity. These are unchanged paid facilities in a more productive order, not additional starting cargo. Missing actual support still invokes the ordinary recovery policy.

- `core_replication_policy: worker_only` leaves the core on its paid worker recipe. The empty core-replication recipe list deliberately prevents the older universal-replicator planner from assuming the core can manufacture missing industry inputs. Those goods require real specialist facilities or paid trade.
- A planned generator receives emergency finished-material funding only when the actual grid has unmet passive demand. With adequate supply, an installed upstream refinery may instead import cheaper ore for the same construction bill. This prevents a nonessential generator from starving existing industry while accumulating a full finished-alloy shipment.
- `fuel_import_buffer_cycles` and `fuel_import_refill_fraction` keep four operating fuel buffers and refill below half that target, avoiding tiny repeated imports. Consumption and physical delivery remain unchanged.
- `bulk_input_policy: remaining_output_bill` derives useful raw feedstock from outstanding manufactured-output needs, counts coproducts once, and caps purchases by that bill, credits, port mass and free volume. Ordinary raw refills use the greater of the AI cycle buffer and the simulation's two-haul `ProductionInputBuffer`; manufactured inputs retain their cycle buffers.
- `export_policy: surplus_shipment_value` compares actual legal shipment quotes. `export_reserve_horizon: next_prerequisite` protects the next required building instead of hoarding its entire future city plan, so a finite starting colony can sell excess manufactured goods to buy a missing part. `full_plan` remains selectable for comparison. Both horizons also protect unfinished construction, any explicitly enabled road/connector bill, current operating/AI reserves and configured worker-reserve inputs. Discrete workers are excluded. Physical loading, local claims, flight time and real credit settlement apply.
- `support_recovery_policy: restore_capacity_before_expansion` restores missing configured capacity ahead of ordinary expansion without buying duplicate pending bays. A temporary power outage in sufficient intact support instead leaves grid recovery available.
- `defense_recovery_policy: first_planned_tower_before_exposure` funds the first required tower before civilian expansion when background bugs or periodic attacks are enabled. Peaceful scenarios keep the economic order. Subsequent owned civilian losses fund one additional tower at a time, bounded by the authored final milestone. Every tower requires ordinary materials, construction and permanent staffing; no guards are invented. An unaffordable tower cannot preempt restoring a trading port when no intact port can fund it. `first_planned_tower_after_civilian_loss` and `ordered_plan_only` retain delayed or unmodified ordering for comparisons. Charging recovery remains first.
- `production_priority_policy: protect_development_inputs` throttles a specialist when its current output already covers planning/worker buffers and its next inputs compete with an unfunded development bill. Once a queued transport has every ingredient locally delivered, the same policy temporarily pauses optional specialist production, universal replication and new trade transactions while its real grid accumulates the 60 kWh assembly payment. Existing paid shipments, essential worker fabrication, charging, generator fuel production and defensive energy priority remain operational. This energy wait requires sufficient actual battery capacity and positive generation after optional idle loads; it ends when the normal factory commits the payment. No charge is granted or reserved in the engine. Both forms of throttling use ordinary building switches: permanent crew, physical inventories and any paid batch escrow stay intact. A fresh AI controller derives the pause and resumption from saved job/inventory state. A deliberately idle staffed factory still satisfies the ordered building plan. `continuous` retains the comparison behavior; player colonies are not throttled by the AI controller.
- `logistics` sets an ordinary vehicle-factory target and two live cargo transports. Once its factory is complete and staffed, the controller queues unmet growth or replacement through the paid fabrication system, even while the later city plan remains unfinished. Uncommitted transport bills precede discretionary expansion; real support and road recovery retain priority. Pending jobs count toward the target; committed material is already held in fabrication escrow. Trade and production fund the actual uncommitted bill. The carried starting transport remains finite, and the planner does not spawn replacement vehicles directly.
- Ingredients already delivered to an uncommitted transport job count toward that job exactly once. They remain unavailable to unrelated construction, and other fabrication demand, repairs, accepted construction and physical pickup claims retain their reservations. A missing transport ingredient with no staffed installed producer receives direct-import priority over unrelated routine feedstock. It still requires actual credits, a paid shipment and physical delivery to the factory.
- When construction goals are satisfied, trade, production and export protection use the next eligible upgrade bill plus the configured operating reserves. Solar generation, the trading port, defense towers and the vehicle factory can progress through their authored levels. Previously upgrade orders could only spend accidental leftovers after the initial plan. Quiet-area requirements for defense upgrades and all normal construction, shipment, staffing and storage constraints still apply.
- Placement preflights a real worker route to the proposed reserved plot while roads are deferred. Legal access must remain physically traversable; purchasing still checks the full bill. `prefer_covered_approaches` favors sampled approach coverage from existing fixed guns while respecting structure occlusion. Armed plots search the configured ring radii and bearings for the greatest additional coverage of exposed building approaches, including the intended mine. `defense_distance` remains the initial authored radius, rather than a restriction that prevents a tower from covering the outside of a more distant civilian plot. Existing and planned guns inform marginal coverage; candidate plots still require legal reserved footprints, deposit clearance and physical access. Open construction phases do not count as finished opaque shells before their authored enclosure phase. Ties and an uncovered-free colony keep the authored spread from the export deposit. This cannot guarantee all-angle protection; `first_legal` remains a comparison policy.

Guard orders apply only to actual existing combat fleets. The current starting manifest carries one unarmed cargo transport and does not create a combat fleet. Visible danger takes precedence over a new worksite. The fleet returns below 30% battery and normally resumes at 90%; visible danger can interrupt charging only when all guards exceed the return threshold. Travel, clear firing positions, ammunition and shot energy remain ordinary simulation behavior. No hidden enemy knowledge or defensive reinforcements are granted.

Before upgrades, the live plan provides approximately 105.92 kW cycle-average generation at full staffing and adequate fuel, with 145 kWh storage. The six industrial recipes would require approximately 921.86 kW at continuous full cadence, before vehicle fabrication, defense and charging. This is a partial-throughput prototype economy, not a promise that every factory runs continuously. The enabled core's fusion source is independent of daylight and crew attendance; worker manufacturing still requires its ordinary physical operating crew.

## Validation and persistence

With roads disabled, workers and cargo vehicles use the shared physical terrain solver. Deep water and excessive slopes remain impassable; authored shallow gravel fords connect river banks. Reserved plots and deposits still require dry land. Placement rejects enclosing active workers or cargo vehicles and preflights access to new AI sites, including the actual operating and construction stations in both directions. If a deposit-center entrance lies on an impassable bank, the extractor searches nearby plots inside the authored extraction snap radius; every candidate must still bind that same deposit and pass normal geometry, access and payment checks. A visibility-graph route that detours outside the local terrain-search envelope is compared with the bounded local contour search; its valid distant route remains available if no shorter contour exists. Loading, unloading and construction assignments try reachable existing crew instead of retaining an inaccessible first candidate. A handler whose real movement fails releases its claim so another reachable worker can service the same truck. Cargo remains in its source, carrier or destination ledger throughout recovery.

Charging is a physical dependency of construction and trade. Before placing an AI worksite, every actual construction/operator station must have a real return route through an existing charger's queue and docking positions. Terrain travel time plus the prospective queue must fit the minimum authored charge reserve; a technically reachable plot with a kilometre-scale detour is rejected before reserving materials. The core's automatic berth supports bootstrap, and service bays require their real permanent attendant. The current candidate also replaces straight-line departure timing for all workers with a cached estimate of actual terrain travel and queue time. Stationary workers reuse the estimate; moving workers conservatively add time to retrace their physical route. Geometry, crew, charger eligibility and queue-slot changes invalidate it, and approaching the charge reserve triggers a fresh estimate before committing a trip. Planning decisions and unavailable-route retries use the existing authored cadence anchored to saved simulation time, so discarding a derived cache cannot shift a recharge decision after loading. This cache is absent from saved worker ledgers; loading recomputes it. The focused `Seige.Workers.ChargingPlansForRealDetours` regression covers cache reuse, load invalidation, uninterrupted-versus-restored physical charging, a real long return without depletion or teleporting, and rejection of an unaffordable departure. Consult the release verification record for results from the final candidate.

A chosen bay must have enough real grid energy for the next charging slice; a completed but disconnected bay is not a destination. Both the queue position and berth approach must be reachable. Service attendants prefer their own self-service berth, and an attendant needed by accepted customers is not lent to cargo work. A power interruption releases an energized worker's charging trip at its current position so it can seek another powered bay. A depleted body waiting outside a berth cannot reserve its entry; a body depleted before docking releases admission in place while keeping its cargo. New guests require an actual attendant with enough reserve to finish their session. Construction assignments retain their slots while builders charge, and returning builders cannot exceed building or road crew capacity. An outpost beyond full-battery range still needs a nearer powered charger. No recovery grants battery energy, relocates a body, or refunds carried cargo.

`node Tools/validate_configuration.mjs` validates the live definition set. `node Tools/test_ai_configuration.mjs` includes malformed established-manifest cases. Native tests must additionally exercise terrain-dependent layouts, distinct body accounting, save/load, deterministic state and continued live operation. `Diagnostics.StartingAIProgress -StartingAILiveTerrain -StartingAIHours=12 -StartingAISeed=815 -StartingAISector=4` runs the real scenario initializer with the game terrain sampler and writes milestone and full-state reports under `Saved/Diagnostics/StartingAI`. `Seige.AI.FiniteKitIndustryOnGameTerrain` requires finite-kit colonies to physically produce components within 24 simulated hours across a center observer and two neighboring sectors; it also checks paid body creation, identity, at least one completed loaded truck delivery at a valid service berth, and validated reload. An unpicked cancellation, cargo loss, return-to-source trip or mere distance traveled does not satisfy the truck-delivery check. These checks use the live grading and slope rules rather than the isolated simulation's flat base. A bounded continuation test is not an indefinite survival guarantee.

Decision timing derives from saved simulation time and state. Save/load initializes definitions with `bDeveloped=false`, then restores the existing snapshot; it does not apply the manifest again. Changing either AI JSON file changes the configuration fingerprint. Runtime validation rejects invalid references, quantities, layout, stock capacity, worker assignments and unsupported modes atomically.

The separate `Seige.AI.FiniteKitSelfDevelopmentOnGameTerrain` acceptance extends the center-colony run to at most 72 simulated hours. It requires actual component, conductor, substrate and circuit output, a completed vehicle factory, a physically fabricated second cargo transport, at least ten successful loaded deliveries and validated reload. It tracks new transport identities against previously committed paid fabrication jobs, alongside the existing worker ledger. This is an acceptance requirement rather than a claim of a passed run; consult the release verification record for measured results. Focused decision tests additionally cover exact upgrade funding, protected exports, duplicate-free paid transport replacement before completion of the city plan, measured power-shortage funding, bounded defense after a civilian loss, and production throttling with preserved batch escrow and resumption. The static AI validator includes 78 negative cases.

An explicit `Diagnostics.StartingAIRestore -StartingAISnapshot="absolute/path/to/snapshot.json" -StartingAISeed=815 -StartingAISector=4 -StartingAIHours=6` diagnostic reloads an existing colony onto the matching real terrain and advances its ordinary simulation until a newly paid transport is completed or the additional time limit expires. It writes to a separate `-restored` journal directory and retains identity, stock and reload checks. This opt-in investigation neither changes nor replaces the fresh 72-hour acceptance; no regular test depends on an external snapshot.

The hostile `Seige.Simulation.FirstPlayableSolvable` acceptance also has a 72-simulated-hour limit, retaining its original finite kit, both threat systems, survival requirement, completed component works and actual manufactured-component goal. The earlier 18-hour milestone is reported explicitly, not claimed as passed. After correcting physical logistics and defense defects, unchanged diagnostic continuations of the failed 18-hour colony completed its replacement charging station around 21.1 hours and alloy refinery around 33.8 hours. At 36 hours it was still funding the component works. Five destroyed structures had already consumed installed materials worth at least 1.5107 credits at ordinary import prices, excluding lost inventories; the single biomass mine's theoretical maximum export revenue is 0.36 credits/hour before actual hauling, staffing and charging losses. These are pacing evidence, not a successful fresh acceptance result. `Diagnostics.HostileFundingReplay -HostileFundingSnapshot="absolute/path/to/hostile-save.json" -HostileFundingHours=6` records bounded unchanged continuations and stock deficits under `Saved/Diagnostics/HostileFunding`; it cannot replace the fresh release gate. No production suppression, faster extraction, cheaper buildings or free replacement units were added to force the former deadline.

## Historical evidence

The paid-growth experiments through run21 exposed real transport, reservation and combat defects, several of which now have focused regressions. They did not validate indefinite AI survival or the replacement established initializer. Those logs and snapshots remain under `Saved/Automation` and `Saved/Diagnostics`.

The [v0.8 record](../docs/verification/v0.8.0.json) includes the earlier 20-installation paid preparation. Their rules, worker model and acceptance scope differ from the v0.10.10 implementation; they are historical evidence only.
